Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Keeper is a voice recorder for iPhone. The short version: Keeper does not collect your data. Your recordings, transcripts and summaries stay on your device.

What Keeper stores, and where

Recordings, their titles, transcripts and summaries are saved as files on your iPhone, inside the app's own folder. You can see the audio files in the Files app. They leave your device only when you choose to share or export them, or through your own device backup (for example iCloud Backup, if you have it turned on).

What Keeper sends to us

Nothing. Keeper has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking software. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings or any information about how you use the app.

Microphone

Keeper uses the microphone only while you are recording.

Transcripts and summaries

Transcripts are produced by Apple's speech recognition running on your iPhone. Summaries are produced by Apple's on-device language model. Keeper requires on-device processing for both, so the audio and text are not sent to us or to any other company for this purpose. The first time you transcribe, your iPhone may download a language model from Apple.

Purchases

Keeper Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account.

Children

Keeper does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a recording inside the app to remove it and its transcript and summary. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
