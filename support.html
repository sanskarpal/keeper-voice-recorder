A recording is missing

Open Keeper, tap the gear, then Find missing recordings. Keeper rescans its folder and puts back any audio file that is not in your list. You can also look in the Files app under On My iPhone › Keeper › Recordings.

The app closed while I was recording

Open Keeper again. The recording appears at the top of the list with a title starting "Recovered".

Transcribe says it is not available

Transcription runs on your iPhone and needs a language model from Apple. Connect to the internet once and try again so the model can download. After that it works offline.

Summarize says it is not available

Summaries need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

I paid but Pro is not active

Tap the gear, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Keeper › Cancel.
